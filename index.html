About

I'm a third-year MBA Tech student in Data Science at NMIMS Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management & Engineering in Mumbai, currently in semester five. My coursework leans on statistics and inference, and I enjoy it most when a formula turns into something I can actually use.

Research and development is where I do my best work: reading around a problem, trying an approach, breaking it, and trying a better one. I'm also the person who keeps a team on schedule. Time management and clear communication are habits I've worked on deliberately.

Outside of code I read psychology, which is a big part of why I'm good at reading people. I also paint, and that side of me has its own section below.
